The Privacy Portal app can be installed on any device. It uses open web standards and is not restricted by any proprietary app store.

## Install on iOS

1. Using your favorite browser, open the app page **[app.privacyportal.org](https://app.privacyportal.org)**

2. Press on the share button

![Press on the share button](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/ios/step_1.png)

3. Press on "Add to Home Screen"

![Press on Add to Homescreen](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/ios/step_2.png)

4. Press on "Add"

![Press on Add](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/ios/step_3.png)

5. Find the Privacy Portal app on your Homescreen

![Find the app on your homescreen](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/ios/step_4.png)

## Install on Android

1. Using your favorite browser, open the app page **[app.privacyportal.org](https://app.privacyportal.org)**

2. Press on the "Install App" button

![Press on install app](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/android/step_1.png)

3. Press on "Install"

![Press on install](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/android/step_2.png)

4. Press on "Add to home screen"

![Press on add to home screen](/assets/blog/how-to-install-the-privacy-portal-app/android/step_3.png)

5. Find the Privacy Portal app on your Homescreen
