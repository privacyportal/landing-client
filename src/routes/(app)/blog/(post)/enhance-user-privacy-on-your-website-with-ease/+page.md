In today’s digital age, privacy is more important than ever. As website owners, we have a responsibility to protect our users’ personal information. But implementing privacy features can feel overwhelming, especially if you’re not a tech expert. That’s why we’re thrilled to announce [privacy-kit](https://github.com/privacyportal/privacy-kit). A Free and Open Source library that makes it incredibly simple to enhance privacy on your website — without any complicated setups or steep learning curves.

## Two Powerful Privacy Features, Made Simple:

This new tool lets you add two game-changing privacy options to your website with almost no effort:

### 1- Hide-My-Email Functionality

Imagine your users filling out a form without worrying about sharing their real email address. With just one small tweak, you can enable a feature that lets them use an email alias instead. Their personal information stays safe, and they feel more confident engaging with your site.

![Hide My Email](/assets/blog/enhance-user-privacy-on-your-website-with-ease/hide-my-email.png)

### 2- Subscribe Anonymously Button

Do you run a newsletter? Now you can add a button that lets users subscribe without revealing their personal email addresses. It’s a quick addition that encourages more sign-ups by giving your audience the peace of mind they deserve.

![Subscribe Anonymously](/assets/blog/enhance-user-privacy-on-your-website-with-ease/subscribe-anonymously.png)

## Easy to Use, No Expertise Required

Best of all, it won’t slow down your site — it’s lightweight and efficient, blending seamlessly into what you’ve already built.

## Build Trust with Your Users

By offering these privacy options, you’re doing more than just protecting your users — you’re showing them you care. When people know their personal information is safe, they’re more likely to interact with your website. That means more form submissions, more newsletter subscribers, and happier visitors overall. It’s a simple way to boost engagement while building trust.

## Communication Made Simple

You might be thinking, “If my users use email aliases, how will I reach them?” Here’s the great part: you can still communicate with them through those aliases. Their real email addresses stay private, but you stay connected. It’s a win-win for everyone.
