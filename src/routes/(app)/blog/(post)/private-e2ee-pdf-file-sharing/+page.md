## Privacy Drawbacks of Conventional File Sharing Methods

All existing file sharing solutions come with privacy and security compromises:

1. **Email Sharing:**

- Privacy Leak: Exposes your email address to the recipient.
- Data Retention: Stores copies of your file in email servers of both sender and receiver, potentially accessible by third parties. The file gets retained and is indefinitely accessible in the recipient's mailbox.

2. **Encrypted Messaging Apps:**

- Personal Information Exposure: Often requires sharing your phone number.
- Data Storage: Despite encryption, files are stored on the app's servers and the recipient's device, increasing the risk of data breaches.
- Data Retention: Often the file is indefinitely retained and accessible by the recipient.
- Compatibility: Requires the recipient to have an account with the messaging app in question.

3. **USB Device Sharing:**

- Security Risks: Transfers via USB can expose your device to malware or unauthorized access, risking not just the file but your entire system.

4. **File Sharing Apps:**

- Identity Exposure: Often exposes your personal details like your name and email address to the recipient and to the service.
- Data Persistence: Even in the best scenarios, your files are stored for a period of time on the service's servers.

Each of these methods compromises your privacy in various ways, highlighting the need for a solution like Privacy Portal's File Sharing, which prioritizes your privacy at every step.

## How does Privacy Portal's File Sharing Work?

1. **Select Your File:** Choose the PDF you wish to share securely.

The file remains in memory and is deleted once the browser tab is closed unless the recipient explicitly chooses to save it.

This process ensures that your file sharing is not only private but also controlled, giving both the sender and the recipient full authority over the data's lifecycle.
